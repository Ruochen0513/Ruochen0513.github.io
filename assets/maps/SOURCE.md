# World map data

The pixel land silhouette in `world-land.svg` is generated from Natural Earth 1:110m land data.

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

License: Public domain. https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Projection: equirectangular, longitude −180…180°, latitude −60…85°. Land outlines only; no political borders.
